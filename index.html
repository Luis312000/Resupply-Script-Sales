FAQ · Payment Oct 21, 2026

Does the donor's credit card information get stored in our system?

Use when: the donor hesitates or asks about card security during the payment step.

"No, ReSupply will not store your credit card information. All payment details are securely handled and stored by our trusted payment processor, Stripe, which is fully PCI compliant. This means your information is encrypted and protected according to the highest industry standards. We only use the card information for this specific pickup, and it will not be used for any other purpose."

Note: If the donor prefers not to proceed with payment, that's completely okay. However, explain that without a valid payment method on file, we're unable to confirm the booking.
Rebuttal · Objection Oct 21, 2026

"Can I drop off the items in this case?"

Use when: the donor wants to avoid the pickup fee and asks to drop off the items themselves.

"Dropping off the items is definitely an option. However, we do try to save you time and effort for the reason that some charities have restrictions with some of the items that are being donated. Therefore, you run the risk of going back home with some items if they are not accepted by the charities for some reason."

"We have a system where the haulers are knowledgeable of which items are not accepted by the charities in the area. So, if they know that no charity would accept the items, we would take the items to a recycling center for a small recycling fee. But again, this is optional so it would be up to you if you want to add that one item to the list or not. In other words, we will be taking care of everything."

SALVO version: "If the items are not accepted by the charity, then we would take them to a recycling center for a small additional fee. It would be up to you if you want to add it to the list or not."
🚫 For SALVO calls, we CANNOT mention the partnership with other charities.

💳 How the donor pays — step by step

Stay on the line while the donor completes these steps on their own screen. Click an image to enlarge it.

  1. Donor opens the email or text they just received and taps the Payment Setup button.
    • If they can't find it, ask them to check Spam/Junk and confirm the email and phone number on file.
  2. The page "Reserve your donation pickup" opens. Have them check the Order preview: pickup date & time and pickup address (editable with Change).
  3. Review the price breakdown together.
    • Booking Fee (Due Today) is the only charge today. It matches Today's Charges.
    • Estimated Pickup Cost (Due On-Site) is paid at the pickup.
  4. Check the box: ☐ I understand my card is only being charged a reservation fee at this time…
    • It sits above the card fields. They must tick it before entering the card.
  5. Enter the card information: card number, expiration date (MM / YY), security code (CVC) and ZIP code.
  6. Tap Reserve Your Pickup (green button at the bottom).
  7. Confirm it went through. Ask the donor if the reservation was completed before moving on to the Recap.
🔒 If the donor is worried about security: payments are processed by Stripe and ReSupply doesn't store the card. See the FAQ in Quick Access.
⚠️ The box in step 4 is required. If "Reserve Your Pickup" doesn't work, check that it is ticked and all card fields are filled in.
